Route 01 · Inline CXL 3.x Sub-Flit Capability Gate

A physical lock on the memory wire that software hacks cannot turn off.

Kill the Bad Write, Not the Whole Wire.

Beat 1 · Motivation

A 64-byte CXL 3.x flit carries four 16-byte tenant slots side-by-side every 4.0 ns. Legacy whole-flit CRC poisoning wrecks all four slots whenever one rogue write arrives—collapsing shared link goodput to 48.90% at a 0.8% bad-slot arrival rate.

Beat 2 · Plain Physical Meaning

Our 2-cycle (8.0 ns) gate splits each slot through a physical LUT6_2 mask, pulling only the bad 16-byte slot’s byte strobes (WSTRB[15:0] = 16'h0000) to silent CXL_NOP at 99.20% goodput while locking revocation in CSR 0x70.

Beat 3 · Scope & Price Paid

CXL IDE (AES-GCM) still encrypts the physical cable; Spectral enforces tenant capability isolation inside the flit stream. Inline emergency revocation buffers d_rev ≤ 8 roots in CSR 0x60 across the ≤ 548 ns dual-bank drain before shadow Bezout deflation.

CXL IDE encrypts physical SerDes lanes between endpoints. Spectral operates inside the decrypted 512-bit flit stream to isolate 64 tenants in shared HBM pools without SRAM page tables.
