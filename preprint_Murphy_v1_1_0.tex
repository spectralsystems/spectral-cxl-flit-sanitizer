\section{Introduction: Adversarial Self-Critique and Why Part~II Exists}
\label{sec:intro}

In classical mathematical and systems publishing, when a foundational hardware-verified construction (Part~I) exposes deeper algebraic questions and boundary assumptions under post-release scrutiny, the clean scholarly response is not to pad Part~I, but to issue a self-contained companion monograph (Part~II) that takes Part~I's sealed silicon receipts as immutable axioms and closes every open lifecycle proof obligation.

In Part~I (\textsc{Spectral Flit-Filter} \BaselineVersion~\cite{murphy2026part1}), we designed, synthesized, and verified on live 16nm AWS EC2 \texttt{f2.6xlarge} HBM2 silicon (\texttt{\AwsAgfiId} and \texttt{\AwsHbmAgfiId}) and in the 7nm FinFET \texttt{ASAP7} predictive PDK~\cite{clark2016asap7} a cut-through Layer-1/2 capability sanitizer~\cite{rose2020zta} for disaggregated \mbox{CXL~3.0--4.0}~\cite{cxl2025spec,dassharma2024cxl,li2023pond,sun2023demystifying} and \mbox{UCIe~1.1--3.0}~\cite{ucie2025spec30} pools. Part~I eliminated the $1.5625\%$ ($250\text{~GB/rack}$) DRAM carve-out and $32$--$68\text{~ns}$ cache-miss stalls of stateful permission tables (\textit{Space-Control}~\cite{goswami2026spacecontrol}), the $12.5\%$ tag carve-out of \textit{CHERI / Morello}~\cite{woodruff2014cheri,watson2015cheri}, multi-cycle offloaded memory re-encryption overheads~\cite{li2025aiore,xu2024dataenclave}, and the $51.1\%$ Go-Back-N replay collapse of tail-CRC poisoning, using \CoreClbLuts\ CLB LUTs and $0\text{~BRAM}$.

Yet as Faraday~\cite{faraday1854mental} and Feynman~\cite{feynman1974cargocult} emphasize, a researcher's primary obligation after building a working instrument is to try ruthlessly to falsify its underlying theory. Subjecting Part~I's \LeanPartOneTotal\ formal theorems (\texttt{thm00}--\texttt{thm29}) and operational claims to adversarial cross-examination uncovered eight substantive lifecycle omissions---alongside four analytical traps in our own preliminary scratch notes (Table~\ref{tab:falsification_ledger}) and frozen baseline constraints (Table~\ref{tab:v100_baseline}):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{The Missing Information-Theoretic Foundation:} Part~I demonstrated \emph{how} 64-bit registers check cohorts in $0\text{~BRAM}$, but never answered how $64\text{~bits}$ of controller state can police an arbitrarily large memory pool $M \to \infty$ without violating Pigeonhole counting lower bounds, nor separated stochastic mean queue occupancy from deterministic worst-case degree bounds.
  \item \textbf{Selective Polynomial Root Reclamation:} Part~I (\texttt{thm20}) proved monotonic root insertion into the degree-8 $\mathbb{F}_{2^8}[Z]$ Vieta annihilator $\Lambda^{(t)}(Z)$, but could not reclaim an individual cohort $\alpha_j$ that had finished cache shootdown without wiping newer mid-shootdown roots or violating active-bank \texttt{SVA 1}.
  \item \textbf{Mode-C Reverse Snoop Synthesis \& Perpetual Uptime:} Part~I bounded \texttt{S2M\_BISnp} invalidation to $\le 500\text{~ns}$, but omitted how the Home Agent synthesizes Feistel-sealed Mode-C wire addresses in $0$ duplicate BRAM after Cycle~0 stripped the upper $(c_{\mathrm{bits}}+g)$ HPA bits, and how a degree-8 polynomial runs indefinitely without saturating at $d_{\mathrm{rev}}=8$.
