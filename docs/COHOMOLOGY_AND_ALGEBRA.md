## 1. Kirchhoff Cycle-Space Decomposition & Fundamental-Chord Slicing (Theorem 4.1)
Let $G = (V, E)$ be a non-tree CXL 3.0–5.0 Port-Based Routing (PBR) switch fabric (or the bipartite König incidence graph $G_{\text{inc}} = (V_H \sqcup E_H, I)$ of a multi-pin interconnect hypergraph $H$ with hyperedge potential $\mathcal{U}^{(p)}(x) = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{h \in E_H} \min_{u \in \mathbb{R}} \|W_h^{(p)}(x_h - u\mathbf{1}_h)\|_\infty^2 + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{(i,j)\in E_{\text{dir}}} w_{ij}(x_i - x_j)_+^2$). Decomposing $\mathbb{R}^{|E|} = \text{im}(B^T) \oplus \ker(B)$ in $O(m)$ time via a minimum-weight spanning forest $T_{\text{forest}} \subset E$ yields $|V| - \beta_0$ acyclic tree branches $e \in T_{\text{forest}}$ and $\beta_1 = |E| - |V| + \beta_0 > 0$ fundamental cycle chords $e^* \in E \setminus T_{\text{forest}}$.

By routing multi-bit payloads along $T_{\text{forest}}$ while enforcing Johnson–Nyquist thermal dithering $n_{\text{th}}(t) \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_{\text{dither}}^2)$, full-wave rectification $y \mapsto |y|$, 1-bit hard slicing $\tilde{L}_{i,j}^{(t)} = V_{\text{dd}}\,\text{sign}(L_{i,j}^{(t)})$ (rank-1 Belief-Propagation antiprojector annihilation $\mathbb{I} - \mathcal{P}_{e^*} = 0$), and derangement coset permutations $\pi_t \in S_n \setminus \text{Aut}(C)$ on all fundamental chords $e^* \in E \setminus T_{\text{forest}}$:
1. **Loop Correlation Annihilation:** Every cyclic loop correlation $W_S = (\prod_{v=1}^{|V|} T_v)(\prod_{e \in S}(\mathbb{I} - \mathcal{P}_e))(\prod_{e \notin S} \mathcal{P}_e)$ ($|S| \ge 3$) vanishes identically ($W_S \equiv 0$).
2. **PF-ODE Score Collapse:** Non-invertible folding $y \mapsto |y|$ collapses each signed equivalence class $\{s \odot y : s \in \{\pm 1\}^n\}$ of cardinality $2^n$ onto a single point, driving continuous-time PF-ODE denoisers to a zero-gradient fixed point $\hat{\epsilon}_\theta(|y|, s(y)) \approx 0$.

## 2. Tarski-Sheaf Hodge-0 Laplacian & Global Section Consistency (Theorem 4.2 & Lemma 9.3)
Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a cellular sheaf over $G$ taking values in the category $\text{DLat}_{\text{conn}}$ of finite bounded distributive security lattices $(L \cong \mathcal{O}(\mathcal{J}(L)) \hookrightarrow \{0,1\}^w, \preceq, \sqcap, \sqcup)$ via Birkhoff's downset embedding $\downarrow(x) = \{p \in P : p \preceq x\}$, equipped with Galois connections $(\alpha_{v \trianglelefteq e}^*, \alpha_{v \trianglelefteq e}^\dagger) : L_v \leftrightarrows L_e$. The **Tarski Hodge-0 Laplacian** $\mathcal{L}_{\text{Tarski}}^0 : C^0(G; \mathcal{F}) \to C^0(G; \mathcal{F})$ is:

$$(\mathcal{L}_{\text{Tarski}}^0 x)_v := \bigsqcap_{e \unrhd v} \alpha_{v \trianglelefteq e}^\dagger \left( \bigsqcap_{u \trianglelefteq e} \alpha_{u \trianglelefteq e}^*(x_u) \right), \qquad H_{\text{Tarski}}^0(G; \mathcal{F}) := \text{Fix}(\mathbb{I} \sqcap \mathcal{L}_{\text{Tarski}}^0)$$

Instantiating the single-cycle combinational Tarski holonomy ratchet $\tau_{e^*}^{\text{out}} = \tau_{e^*}^{\text{in}} \sqcap \Gamma_{e^*}$ where $\Gamma_{e^*} := \alpha_{u \trianglelefteq e^*}^*(x_u) \sqcap \alpha_{v \trianglelefteq e^*}^*(x_v) \sqcap \bigsqcap_{w \in C(e^*)} \rho_{e^* \leftarrow w}(x_w)$ strips simple and composite cyclic Confused-Deputy privilege escalations in $O(1)$ gate depth.
