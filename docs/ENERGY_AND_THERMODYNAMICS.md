## 1. Kron Schur-Complement Dirichlet & Finite-Time Landauer Bounds (Theorem 6.1 & Lemma 9.6)
Let the grounded-Dirichlet network Graph Laplacian $\tilde{L}_G = L_G + G_{V_{\text{SS}}} > 0$ be partitioned into boundary ports $B$ and interior nodes $S$ with Kron Schur complement $S_\Gamma = \tilde{L}_{B,B} - \tilde{L}_{B,S}\tilde{L}_{S,S}^{-1}\tilde{L}_{S,B} > 0$ and effective-resistance leverage scores $\ell_e = w_e^\Gamma (\delta_a - \delta_b)^T S_\Gamma^{-1}(\delta_a - \delta_b)$:
1. **Perimeter Lower Bound on Interior Dissipation:** By block LDU factorization and Cheeger's inequality $\lambda_2(S_\Gamma) \ge \frac{1}{2}h^2(S_\Gamma)$, any interior state vector $u_S \in \mathbb{R}^{|S|}$ induced by boundary toggles $\Delta B[n]$ dissipates power bounded below in $O(1)$ time by:
   $$\dot{Q}_{\text{interior}}[n] \ge \dot{Q}_{\text{perimeter}}[n] = (k_B T_0 \ln 2) f_{\text{clk}} \sum_{e \in E(S_\Gamma)} \ell_e \Delta H_e[n] \ge (k_B T_0 \ln 2) f_{\text{clk}} \frac{h^2(S_\Gamma)}{2 d_{\max}} \|\Delta B[n]\|_2^2$$
2. **Benamou–Brenier Wasserstein-2 Finite-Time Erasure & Non-Latching Load-Line Condition:** Erasing or transitioning a symmetric zero-mean state density $p_0(x)$ over finite time $\tau$ with error $\epsilon \in [0, 1/2)$ requires minimum work:
   $$\frac{W_{\min}(\tau, \epsilon)}{k_B T} \ge \ln 2 + \epsilon \ln \epsilon + (1-\epsilon)\ln(1-\epsilon) + \frac{1}{2D\tau}\int_0^{F_0^{-1}(1-\epsilon/2)} 2 p_0(x) x^2\,dx \xrightarrow{\epsilon \to 0} \ln 2 + \frac{\text{Var}(x)}{2D\tau}$$
   In a correlated-electron $\text{V}_{1-x}\text{W}_x\text{O}_2$ load ($x \approx 0.018$) operated within $F \in [2.0, 8.5]\text{ mJ/cm}^2 < F_{\text{SPT}}$, enforcing series resistance $R_s(V_g) > R_s^{\min} = R_{\text{met}}(V_{\text{DD}} - V_L)/V_L$ precludes DC metallic equilibrium latching ($\Delta T_{\text{hyst}} \equiv 0\text{ K}$), while auto-zeroing capacitance $C_{\text{AZ}} \ge \frac{16 k_B T}{\epsilon_{\text{os}}^2}$ ($\approx 1.27\text{ pF}$ at $360\text{ K}$), $T_{\text{epoch}} \ge \frac{C_{\text{AZ}}}{g_{m,N}}\ln(|V_{\text{os,main}}|/\epsilon_{\text{os}})$, and reverse-recovery bias $|V_{\text{rec}} - V_{\text{CM}}| \ge \frac{1}{q\alpha_{\text{ox}}}(E_{a,\max} - k_B T \ln(T_{\text{epoch}}/5\tau_0))$ guarantee $|V_{\text{os,eff}}| \le 0.25\text{ mV}$ and 10-year BTI detrapping (Lemma 9.6).
