# Security, OPSEC & Data Rights Policy

## Zero-Knowledge Repository Scope
This repository hosts strictly public zero-knowledge verification ledgers, untimed behavioral reference models, and machine-checked Microsoft Lean 4 mathematical proofs (`formal_lean4/SheafCosetIsometry.lean`).

All synthesizable SystemVerilog implementations (`*.sv`), 7nm FinFET Liberty timing models (`*.lib`), and placed-and-routed FPGA checkpoints (`*.dcp`) are maintained in an air-gapped vault (and executed in CI exclusively via encrypted ephemeral RAM disks) protected under **SBIR/STTR Data Rights (DFARS 252.227-7018)** and **16 Pending U.S. Provisional Patent Applications** (unpublished serial numbers and specifications withheld in confidence under 35 U.S.C. § 122).

## Vulnerability & Audit Disclosure
Report any mathematical proof or cryptographic ledger anomalies directly to `admin@spectralsystems.org`.
